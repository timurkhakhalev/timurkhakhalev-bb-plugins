Voice input through your Codex sign-in.

## What you get

- A **Codex dictation** voice service in Settings → AI services.
- The microphone shows only while a connected bb machine has Codex signed in with ChatGPT.

## How it works

The plugin reads Codex's own sign-in on the machine and sends the recording to the same ChatGPT transcription endpoint the Codex app uses. Your Codex subscription covers it.
